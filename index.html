The Ideas buttons switch the model to night and stage six concept sketches for the mehfil concert. They are illustrations of ideas, not engineered designs.

Rebuilt from published photographs, written descriptions and the OpenStreetMap footprint. The plan size is measured. Heights, row counts and the lower wings are estimated from photos, so treat this as a faithful sketch and not a survey.
